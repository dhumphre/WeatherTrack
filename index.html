How the hike score is figured

Each day is judged on its daylight hours (7am–7pm, local to the place). It starts at 100 and loses points for the chance of rain or snow, for wind over about 10 mph, for highs above 85°F or below 40°F, for frosty mornings, and extra for thunderstorms, snow and fog. Air quality costs nothing up to an AQI of 50, a little through 100, and a lot above 150, where smoke makes a hike unwise. 80 and up is Prime, 60 is Good, 40 is Fair, 20 is Poor, below that Skip. Places are listed best first for the selected day.

Forecasts: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Air quality: Open-Meteo. Place search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Your places are saved in this browser only.
